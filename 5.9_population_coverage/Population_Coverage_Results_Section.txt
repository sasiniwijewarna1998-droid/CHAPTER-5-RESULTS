5.X Population Coverage Analysis

Population coverage analysis was performed using the IEDB Population Coverage
Tool (v3.0.2) to evaluate the proportion of individuals in target populations
expected to respond to the selected epitope ensemble. The analysis considered
28 HLA Class I alleles (representing 12 MHC-I supertypes: A1, A2, A3, A24,
A26, B7, B8, B27, B39, B44, B58, B62) and 23 HLA Class II alleles (spanning
DRB1, DRB3, DRB4, DRB5, DPA1, and DQA1 loci). Coverage was assessed for four
target populations: World, South Asia, Southeast Asia, and Sri Lanka.

5.X.1 MHC Class I (CTL Epitope) Coverage

The 15 selected CTL epitopes collectively provided high population coverage
across the global population and Southeast Asia (Table 5.X). The world
population showed 98.20% coverage, with an average of 10.13 epitope-HLA
interactions per individual and a PC90 value of 4.24 (i.e., 90% of the
population would recognize at least 4 epitopes). Southeast Asia demonstrated
96.10% coverage (average hits = 9.39, PC90 = 2.96), while South Asia reached
91.18% coverage (average hits = 6.97, PC90 = 2.09). Sri Lanka showed a lower
Class I coverage of 52.39% (average hits = 2.39, PC90 = 0.42); however, this
is attributable to the limited availability of Sri Lankan HLA Class I
frequency data in the IEDB Allele Frequency Database rather than true
immunological insufficiency. India, as a South Asian comparator with more
comprehensive HLA typing data, showed 87.51% Class I coverage.

Table 5.X: MHC Class I Population Coverage of Selected CTL Epitopes

Population          Coverage (%)   Average Hits   PC90
-------------------------------------------------------
World               98.20          10.13          4.24
South Asia          91.18           6.97          2.09
Southeast Asia      96.10           9.39          2.96
Sri Lanka           52.39*          2.39          0.42
India               87.51           6.28          1.60

*Limited HLA frequency data available in IEDB for this population.
PC90: minimum number of epitope-HLA interactions recognized by 90% of the
population.

5.X.2 MHC Class II (HTL Epitope) Coverage

The 15 selected HTL epitopes achieved exceptionally high Class II coverage
across all populations with available data (Table 5.X). Global coverage
reached 99.68%, with an average of 49.87 epitope-HLA interactions per
individual and a PC90 of 34.79. South Asia demonstrated the highest
regional coverage at 99.74% (average hits = 46.11, PC90 = 32.47),
comparable to India alone at 99.74% (average hits = 46.00, PC90 = 32.39).
Southeast Asia showed 94.53% coverage (average hits = 27.81, PC90 = 17.25).
HLA Class II frequency data for Sri Lanka was not available in the IEDB
database; therefore, coverage for this population could not be independently
assessed. Given that South Asia as a whole (which encompasses Sri Lanka)
achieved 99.74% Class II coverage, a similarly high coverage for the Sri
Lankan population is expected.

Table 5.X: MHC Class II Population Coverage of Selected HTL Epitopes

Population          Coverage (%)   Average Hits   PC90
-------------------------------------------------------
World               99.68          49.87          34.79
South Asia          99.74          46.11          32.47
Southeast Asia      94.53          27.81          17.25
Sri Lanka           N/A**          N/A**          N/A**
India               99.74          46.00          32.39

**No HLA Class II frequency data available for Sri Lanka in the IEDB
Allele Frequency Database.

5.X.3 Combined (Class I + II) Population Coverage

When MHC Class I and Class II coverage were combined, near-complete population
coverage was achieved for all regions with comprehensive HLA data (Table 5.X).
The global combined coverage was 99.99%, with South Asia at 99.98% (average
hits = 53.07, PC90 = 32.63) and Southeast Asia at 99.79% (average hits = 37.20,
PC90 = 18.86). The combined coverage for Sri Lanka (52.39%) reflects only
Class I data, as Class II frequency data is unavailable.

Table 5.X: Combined Population Coverage of Selected CTL and HTL Epitopes

Population          Coverage (%)   Average Hits   PC90
-------------------------------------------------------
World               99.99          60.01          37.66
South Asia          99.98          53.07          32.63
Southeast Asia      99.79          37.20          18.86
Sri Lanka           52.39*         2.39           0.42
India               99.97          52.28          32.30

*Combined coverage reflects Class I only due to absence of Class II HLA
data for Sri Lanka.

================================================================================
NOTE ON SRI LANKA DATA LIMITATION (Suggested Discussion paragraph)
================================================================================

The population coverage analysis for Sri Lanka was limited by the scarcity of
HLA frequency data in the IEDB Allele Frequency Database. While Sri Lanka
appears in the database for HLA Class I alleles, no Class II frequency data
is available, and the Class I data itself is based on limited studies. As a
result, the computed coverage for Sri Lanka (52.39% for Class I) likely
underestimates the true coverage. This is supported by the observation that
the broader South Asian population, which includes Sri Lanka, achieved 99.98%
combined coverage. India, as a neighboring South Asian country with more
comprehensive HLA typing, showed 99.97% combined coverage. Future studies
incorporating Sri Lankan-specific HLA frequency data from the Allele Frequency
Net Database or population-based HLA typing studies would provide more accurate
coverage estimates. The high coverage observed across South Asia and Southeast
Asia (the primary dengue-endemic regions of interest) supports the broad
applicability of the selected epitope ensemble.
